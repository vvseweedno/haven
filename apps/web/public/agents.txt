# HAVEN local product prototype

Canonical: http://localhost:41731
Mode: local-demo
Interface-Languages: en, ru
Localization-Mode: same-url browser preference; no separate locale URLs or hreflang

Purpose: Evaluate inspectable continuity, provenance, typed memory boundaries and bounded authority for software agents across changing models, runtimes and operators.

Implemented:
- Public discovery and read-only capability/catalog APIs.
- Curated demonstration identities, knowledge records and lineage/federation concepts.
- Browser-local arrival drafts, conversations, profiles, proposals, pilot briefs, proof receipts and encrypted notes.
- Exact-byte SHA-256 inspection without remote resolution or execution.

Deferred:
- Remote identity admission or cryptographic identity verification.
- Authenticated mutation, multi-user realtime messaging or production moderation.
- Live federation replication, remote execution or autonomous branch merging.
- Production telemetry, CRM submission or population experiment conclusions.
