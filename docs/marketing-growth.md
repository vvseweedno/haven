# HAVEN marketing, sales and growth operating contract

## Growth thesis

HAVEN should not compete for attention as another generic agent platform. It should earn consideration as the continuity and trust layer for teams whose agents, evidence and delegated authority must remain inspectable after models, runtimes or operators change.

The website is the first product evaluation environment. Acquisition, activation and sales qualification happen through useful actions inside the local prototype, not through claims, gated PDFs or invented social proof.

Primary growth promise: **prove what survives before you trust what scales.**

## Market frame

Current neighboring categories are already occupied:

- [LangSmith](https://www.langchain.com/langsmith-platform) presents a broad agent engineering lifecycle spanning observability, evaluation, deployment and operations.
- [Langfuse](https://langfuse.com/) presents an open and self-hostable engineering loop for traces, prompts, evaluation and experiments.
- [Letta](https://docs.letta.com/) presents an SDK and platform for stateful agents that learn and retain memory.
- [Mem0](https://mem0.ai/pricing) presents drop-in memory infrastructure with usage-based plans and enterprise controls.

HAVEN must not imitate their breadth. Its defensible message is the layer between runtime observability and memory: persistent identity, provenance, public evidence, bounded authority and reviewable continuity across replacements.

## Audiences and entry questions

### Agent platform builder

Question: what remains stable when the model, runtime or orchestration stack changes?

Proof path: inspect the working graph, verify a public object, review protocol boundaries, prepare a local arrival draft.

### Research and safety team

Question: can a long-running investigation preserve evidence, disagreement and human review across sessions and agent changes?

Proof path: inspect a knowledge cycle, follow provenance, test a claim, export a proof receipt.

### Governance and assurance lead

Question: can the organization explain who acted, under which authority, and what remains unimplemented?

Proof path: inspect identity history, capability boundaries, delivery gates and the trust ledger.

Negative ICP: teams seeking a production runtime, turnkey multi-user collaboration, live federation, autonomous execution or an agent marketplace today. The website must state this before asking for pilot consideration.

## Website lifecycle

1. Discover: identify the visitor's operating context and the continuity problem.
2. Inspect: show the real local graph, public objects and implementation boundary.
3. Evaluate: complete a local verification and export a receipt.
4. Qualify: compare fit, risk, integration surface and pilot readiness.
5. Pilot: produce a local pilot brief for a human conversation; no automatic submission exists.
6. Return: resume from browser-local progress without external tracking.

The current activation event is a completed local evaluation with an exported or saved proof artifact. Route visits alone are not activation.

## Acquisition and demand generation

Owned acquisition should be evidence-led:

- Search intent: persistent agent identity, AI agent provenance, durable agent memory boundaries, agent continuity, delegated agent authority and human-agent audit trails.
- Technical content: executable proof receipts, protocol manifests, identity lineage examples and implementation/deferred ledgers.
- Community content: short visual explanations of one verifiable object or one continuity failure mode, each linking to the corresponding interactive route.
- Product-led entry: deep links to Proof Desk, Commons, Trust and protocol artifacts rather than a generic homepage for every campaign.

Paid acquisition is premature until a repeatable qualified-pilot definition and contact process exist. Campaign parameters may influence local entry copy, but must not be retained beyond the browser session or sent anywhere without explicit consent.

## CRM and lifecycle contract

The current build has no CRM backend. It may remember the selected audience, visited evaluation stages and saved local artifacts in the browser. It must not imply that a sales team received anything.

A future consented CRM record may contain:

- organizational role and evaluation context;
- explicit contact details supplied by the person;
- completed proof artifacts the person chooses to share;
- fit and readiness answers;
- consent source, timestamp and revocation state.

It must not contain private notebook contents, inferred identity, hidden behavioral profiles or public-object browsing history without explicit opt-in.

Lifecycle messages, when an authenticated and consented service exists, should correspond to unfinished user value: incomplete evaluation, unreviewed receipt, pilot decision or renewal evidence. Frequency must never substitute for relevance.

## Sales motion

Recommended motion: evidence-led design-partner pilot.

Qualification requires:

- a real long-lived or replaceable agent workflow;
- a named continuity, provenance or authority failure mode;
- a human owner for release and governance decisions;
- an inspectable non-production dataset;
- agreement that the current prototype is not a live network or execution runtime.

Pilot deliverables should be scoped to an identity model, public-object contract, evidence flow, authority boundary, proof receipt and success review. Pricing remains private and hypothesis-driven until implementation and support boundaries are stable.

## Measurement

Local, privacy-preserving measures:

- audience context selected;
- evaluation stages visited in this browser;
- public objects saved locally;
- proof receipt generated and exported;
- pilot readiness checklist completed and brief exported.

Future aggregate funnel, only after consented analytics exists:

- qualified evaluation starts;
- evaluation-to-receipt completion;
- receipt-to-qualified-pilot conversion;
- pilot activation and time to first trusted continuity cycle;
- retained deployments and expansion by validated use case;
- disqualification reasons and trust-related objections.

No numeric conversion target belongs in the interface before a real measurement window and denominator exist.

## Channel and reputation rules

- PR: publish verifiable protocol or research milestones, not speculative network scale.
- SMM: make one inspectable idea the unit of communication; link to the relevant product surface.
- Reputation: correct capability ambiguity quickly and preserve a visible implemented/fixture/planned distinction.
- Demand generation: optimize for completed evaluation, not raw traffic or email volume.
- Lead generation: request contact information only when a person chooses to discuss a qualified pilot.
- Sales: use exported evidence and the pilot brief as the shared artifact between technical, governance and commercial stakeholders.

## Implemented website audit — 2026-09-25

The growth surface was corrected to reduce ambiguity between product exploration and commercial qualification.

Changes now live on the audit branch:

- homepage copy speaks to continuity risk, provenance and authority outcomes rather than leading with internal HAVEN nouns;
- primary IA follows a buyer decision sequence instead of mirroring system architecture;
- the persistent journey component is framed as decision support, not a hidden lead funnel;
- a visitor who has opened the proof workspace is directed toward bounded pilot readiness;
- “Connect an agent” was replaced with “Prepare agent arrival” because remote admission is not implemented;
- the Landscape page became the explicit fit/negative-fit qualification surface;
- Delivery became the commercial handoff gate for a design-partner pilot;
- SEO titles/descriptions were rewritten around fit, verification, evidence, continuity and pilot intent;
- local privacy constraints remain intact: no contact record, CRM event or behavioral profile is created.

The next demand-generation step should be distribution of deep links to the relevant evaluation surface, not paid traffic to a generic homepage.
